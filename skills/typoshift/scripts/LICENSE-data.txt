layouts.json is generated from xkeyboard-config (https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/xkeyboard-config/xkeyboard-config)
and xorgproto keysymdef.h; both are under permissive X11/MIT-style licenses. Full notices:
https://github.com/g4lb/typoshift-skill/tree/main/tools/xkb

common_words.json lists the most frequent words per language from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), derived from OpenSubtitles 2018. It is licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: lower-cased, filtered to
letters typeable on each language's keyboard layout, truncated.
